# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS — a single self-contained page plus one image asset, meant to be
hosted as plain files (e.g. Netlify, GitHub Pages). No framework, no build step.

## Users

Friends and family of the couple, Will & Cora, who receive a link and open it on a
phone or laptop. They are not RSVPing for real here — they are being charmed and teased
by someone close to the couple.

## Product Purpose

A playful "save the date" mini-page: show the couple's hand-painted save-the-date
illustration, then run a tongue-in-cheek bit where the guest "decides" to come. It
exists to delight, not to collect data. Success = the guest smiles, plays along, and
remembers the date (16.07.27, Lisbon).

## Positioning

An inside joke made visible: the page is signed by "the first person they met in
Lisbon" and runs a gag RSVP (a "No" button you cannot click; a quiz that can declare
you "uninvited"). The charm is the personal, hand-made voice — not a polished vendor
wedding template.

## Operating Context

Opened from a shared link, most often on a phone, once, for under a minute. The
illustration is the real artifact (provided PDF). The interaction must work by touch:
the "No" button must dodge a finger, not just a hovering mouse.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mechanics to preserve: (1) the save-the-date illustration up top; (2) "Want to come
  to our wedding?" with Yes / No, where No is impossible to click because it moves away
  on both mouse and touch; (3) clicking Yes opens a short multi-question quiz; (4) a
  final verdict, "You passed!" or "You're uninvited!", based on the answers, with a way
  to retry; (5) a closing signature.
- Content is English.
- No real RSVP, no form submission, no backend, no personal data collected.
- Must be a self-contained static deliverable (single HTML + image).

## Brand Commitments

- Couple: Will & Cora. Date: 16.07.27. Place: Lisbon, Portugal.
- The save-the-date illustration (hand-painted view of Lisbon's hills and river, with
  the 25 de Abril bridge and a terracotta-roofed house) is a fixed asset: save-the-date.jpg.
- The closing signature line is intentionally a riddle: "with love, by the first
  person they met in Lisbon." Keep that voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- save-the-date.jpg (rendered from the user's PDF) — the couple's real artwork.
- No other real content exists: no venue details, schedule, registry, or RSVP system.
  Future work must not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Delight over utility — this is a joke that happens to carry a date, not a wedding ops tool.
2. Personal, hand-made voice — it should read as made by a friend, never as a template.
3. Touch-first mischief — every gag must land on a phone first.
4. Preserve the couple's artwork and the riddle signature as fixed anchors.
